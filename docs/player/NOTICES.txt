ATTRIBUTION AND PROVENANCE

Unofficial fan mod by beatsdtx06 for Prince of Persia: The Two Thrones. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Ubisoft, the voice actors, PoP Mod Manager or OpenJade developers.

Game assets, scripts, dialogue and underlying voice performances derive from Ubisoft games. Their rights remain with their respective owners. This source-available mod does not assign an open-source license to Ubisoft content or third-party tools and makes no blanket legal-clearance claim. Use legally obtained game copies.

The converted Prince dialogue uses Warrior Within voice references while retaining The Two Thrones wording and timing. It is not a new recording by, or endorsement from, either original actor. The local workflow used the separate Seed-VC project (Plachtaa/seed-vc). Its code, models, reference dataset and weights are not included in the player ZIP. Gameplay efforts are original WW clips. The Dark Prince and other speakers are not voice-converted.

PoP Mod Manager and the JTMod ecosystem are by kaminoer. OpenJade is a separately installed engine dependency. Their executables, DLLs and implementation source are not included.

This package contains native model record edits, English 001.sb0 and 004.sb0 replacement banks, and wwprince.ls0. Unrelated bank records/data are preserved. No whole BF/BIG game archive, game executable, private backup, API credential or AI model is included. Replacement banks are required by the native manager format; their presence is not a license grant for underlying content.
