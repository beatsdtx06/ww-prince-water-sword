OpenJade.

Game assets, scripts, dialogue and voice performances belong to Ubisoft and

Converted Prince dialogue uses Warrior Within voice references with The Two
Thrones wording and timing, made locally with Seed-VC (Plachtaa/seed-vc). It is
not a new recording by either actor. Seed-VC code, models and weights are not
included. Gameplay efforts are original WW clips.

PoP Mod Manager and the JTMod format are by kaminoer. OpenJade is a separate
engine. Neither is included.
